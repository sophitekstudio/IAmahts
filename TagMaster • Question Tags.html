📘 Règles essentielles

🔹 Base

Affirmatif → tag négatif. Négatif → tag affirmatif. On reprend l'auxiliaire.

You are tired, aren't you?
She doesn't like coffee, does she?

🔸 I am, Let's, impératifs

I am → aren't I ? · Let's → shall we ? · impératif → will you ?

I am late, aren't I?
Let's dance, shall we?
Open the door, will you?

🔹 Nobody / Everybody

Sujet indéfini personne → tag avec they.

Nobody called, did they?
Everyone is here, aren't they?

🔸 There is / This is

There is → isn't there? · This/That → isn't it?

There is a problem, isn't there?
That is your car, isn't it?
